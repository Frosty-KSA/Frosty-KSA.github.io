Projects

Design stage

3D-Printed Subsonic Wind Tunnel

A subsonic wind tunnel designed to be 3D printed. The design uses an open-circuit suction layout, a bell mouth inlet, honeycomb and screens, and a 5th-order contraction. Sizing and fan selection are being reworked before the build starts.

Research plan

ShellMorph: Continuous-Surface Morphing Airfoil

A planned study of an airfoil whose outer shell changes shape between three flight states, to be compared against a conventional airfoil with a flap across takeoff, cruise and landing. Currently at the planning stage.
